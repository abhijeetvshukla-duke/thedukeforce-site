        FAQ
        Questions we hear most.
        
    
        
      
        What do HCM and Workforce Management mean in UKG Ready?
        HCM (Human Capital Management) covers the people side of your business: HR records, onboarding, payroll and reporting. Workforce Management (WFM) covers time: timekeeping, scheduling, attendance and accruals. UKG Ready brings both together in one system, and The Duke Force implements them as one connected build.
        
      
        How long does a UKG Ready implementation take?
        Most mid-size organizations go live with UKG Ready HCM and Workforce Management in 10 to 14 weeks. Multi-state pay rules, complex accruals and data quality shape the timeline, and we map those in the first two weeks.
        
      
        Do you handle multi-state and city-level rules?
        Yes. We configure federal, state and local overtime, break, paid sick leave and scheduling requirements for every location you operate in.
        
      
        Can you fix an implementation someone else did?
        Yes. Our optimization service starts with a full audit, then we fix what matters most first, without disrupting live payrolls.
        
      
        Are you part of UKG?
        No. The Duke Force is an independent consultancy specializing in UKG Ready. We work directly with your team and alongside your UKG contacts where needed.
        
      
        What happens after go-live?
        We stay through your first live payroll cycles, then hand over documentation and admin training. Ongoing changes can be covered by a managed support plan.
